# Build with the life-event-services playbook

1. Read `content/playbooks/life-event-services/playbook.ts`.
2. Read `content/playbooks/life-event-services/life-event-services.data.json`.
3. Read `references/domain-brief.md` completely.
4. Ask what outcome the builder wants to explore before proposing implementation.
5. Separate published facts, project interpretation, synthetic values, and unsupported assumptions in every recommendation.
6. Present at least two credible approaches when implementation direction is open. Include a non-AI or service-design approach when it could meet the outcome.
7. Treat authority, consent, matching errors, accessibility, distress, and fraud exposure as design inputs rather than end-of-plan disclaimers.
8. Do not select a recipient, field set, or solution without authority in a published source or from the responsible source owner.
9. Follow `AGENTS.md`, the typed playbook contract, privacy rules, and repository quality gates before changing code.
10. Stop and name the missing authority when the work requires real bereavement records, production notifications, legal interpretation, or service-owner approval.

Do not claim to replace service owners, legal advisers, registrars, bereavement professionals, affected families, or data controllers.
